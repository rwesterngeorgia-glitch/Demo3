# StockPilot — concept case study

StockPilot is a hospitality inventory and operations prototype developed for our digital agency portfolio. It explores how bars, restaurants, and cafés could bring stock balances, supplier orders, recipe costing, waste, and daily routines into one understandable workspace.

The engineering focus is consistency. Every stock change creates an audit record, transfers preserve company stock, physical counts reconcile discrepancies, purchase deliveries support partial receiving with replay protection, and recipes recalculate from current ingredient prices. The responsive React/TypeScript app includes searchable tables, event-derived reports, CSV exports, a clear interactive tour, light/dark themes, and versioned browser-local persistence.

All records are fictional. There is no live customer, backend, authentication, supplier connection, sales dataset, or measured business outcome. Recipe profitability is theoretical. This concept demonstrates functional software development and provides a concrete starting point for customer discovery, backend integration, and production planning.

Built with React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Recharts. Hosted statically on Cloudflare Pages.
