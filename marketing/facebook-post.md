**Know your stock. Control your costs.**

Introducing StockPilot, a concept/prototype we developed for our agency portfolio to show how custom software can support the everyday work of bars, restaurants, and cafés.

This is a working application: add products, transfer inventory between four locations, receive part of a purchase order, cost a cocktail, record waste, and export reports. Every stock change leaves a before-and-after audit trail. Recipe costs update when ingredient prices change.

Try the interactive demo: https://stockpilot-demo.pages.dev/app

Product overview: https://stockpilot-demo.pages.dev

The guided tour lets you use the real workflows. No account is needed. All business data is fictional and changes stay in your browser. This prototype is not connected to a real business, and its recipe margins are theoretical.

Have an operational workflow that spreadsheets make difficult? This concept illustrates the kinds of practical, custom applications our agency can build around a business’s requirements.
